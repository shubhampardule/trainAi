# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TrainAI to be a project where anyone can ask a question, report that
something broke, or propose a change without worrying about how they'll be
treated. Contributors and maintainers commit to making participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity, or the hardware they happen to own.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a documentation bug, not a person's fault.
- Give feedback on the code, not the coder.
- Accept that people have different levels of ML background. "How does this
  work?" is a legitimate question, and a poor answer to it is a poor answer.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to say no to a change, and a contributor's
  decision to stop working on one.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning remarks, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code — and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a confidential issue with the maintainers, or by
contacting them directly through the address listed on the repository's
maintainer profile. Reports are reviewed as promptly as an all-volunteer project
can manage, and the reporter's identity is kept private.

Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban a participant temporarily or
permanently, in proportion to the behaviour. Maintainers who do not act in good
faith when enforcing this document may themselves face consequences from other
maintainers.
